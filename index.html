We are an indie game company and developer that believes games should have personality. We create with care, experiment without fear, and focus on the details that make an experience memorable.

GiftBox began with the wish to build something of our own: a video game studio for telling stories, testing new ideas, and sharing worlds that could only exist here. In our devlog, we share the process behind each creation.
